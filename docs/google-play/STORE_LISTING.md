## Full description

Deliverr brings your favourite local stores to your door across Regina, Saskatchewan.

Browse beer, wine, spirits, ciders, seltzers, smoke products, groceries, pet supplies and takeout from participating local retailers. Compare available products, choose a delivery time and follow your order from checkout to delivery.

Why customers choose Deliverr:

- Delivery across Regina, Saskatchewan
- Local stores and familiar products in one app
- Convenient scheduled or on-demand delivery windows
- Secure credit and debit card payments
- Pay at the door where available
- Order progress updates and support when you need it
- Saved addresses and faster repeat checkout

Alcohol and tobacco products are available only to customers aged 19 or older. Valid government-issued photo identification is required at delivery. Availability, prices and delivery times vary by store.
